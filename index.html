The model

Your software already knows the answers. We let AI ask the questions.

Most companies don't need another AI product bolted on from the outside. They need their own systems — the databases, APIs, and workflows that run the business — to become things an AI agent can reason about and act on. That bridge is what we build.

MCP servers

We wrap your data and operations in a Model Context Protocol server — a clean, governed interface any AI agent can connect to. Your systems stay where they are; the agent simply gains a vocabulary to use them.

Skills & agents

On top of those tools we author skills — reusable, expert procedures that encode how work should be done — and assemble agents that follow them reliably, with the guardrails your domain requires.

Integrated, not bolted on

Everything runs in your environment, against your live data, under your access rules. The result feels less like a chatbot and more like a capable new colleague who already knows your tools.

Illustrative example A day in the life

A specialty wine importer, suddenly fluent in its own catalogue.

Imagine a mid-sized importer and distributor of fine wines. Decades of knowledge live in an inventory database, a tangle of supplier price lists, and the heads of a few very busy people. Here is what happens once MyHarness wires it together.

Before

  • Stock levels, vintages and allocations live in a system only two people can query confidently.
  • Supplier lead times and minimums are buried in PDFs and email threads.
  • A simple question — "what can I pour for this event, and what should I reorder?" — takes an afternoon.

After MyHarness

  • An MCP server exposes the catalogue, live inventory, supplier terms and order history as governed tools.
  • A reorder-planner skill encodes how the buyer weighs margin, lead time and minimums.
  • Anyone on the team simply asks — in plain language — and gets an answer grounded in real data, plus a draft action.
